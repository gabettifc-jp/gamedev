## 返ってきた設計書（全文・原文のまま）

> **Game promise**
>
> A one-button fishing incremental where every cast resolves on a musical beat. The pleasure comes from a quiet wait snapping into a precise hit, a fish flying onto the dock, and the cash display jumping by a new order of magnitude. Gear breakthroughs and prestige perks alternate deliberate stalls with short, loudly celebrated surges.
>
> **Core loop**
>
> 1. Press the primary action to cast; the bobber arcs into the water.
> 2. Wait 2–8 seconds, quantized to the background track's beat. The bobber twitches before the bite so the player can refocus.
> 3. On the bite, a four-step rhythm bar appears. A fish-shaped cursor advances on three count-in beats; press on the highlighted fourth beat.
> 4. Perfect timing (about ±60 ms for common fish, tightening to ±45 ms for rare fish) pays 1.5×; Good timing (about ±150/110 ms) pays 1×. Outside the Good window or no input snaps the line, awards nothing, and resets the catch streak.
> 5. Spend money between casts on gear levels. Each purchase visibly changes a useful statistic; level milestones create large output jumps and activate a three-catch Golden Current surge.
> 6. When costs outpace earnings, prestige into Tide Stars, spend them on permanent perks, and restart the gear ladder with enough power to break the wall.
>
> A successful streak adds 10% payout per fish up to 2×. This rewards rhythm accuracy without making one miss erase purchased progress. All economy, timing judgments, fish selection, prestige rewards, and ending conditions are deterministic.
>
> **Camera and controls**
>
> * PixiJS 2D, fixed side-on 16:9 composition at a logical 320×180 resolution, integer-scaled with crisp nearest-neighbor pixels.
> * Upper 65%: angler on the left, water and bobber across the center, distant horizon behind. Lower 35%: money and rate readout above a compact gear dock.
> * The rhythm bar overlays the water at screen center and temporarily takes visual priority over upgrades.
> * Keyboard: Space or Enter casts and performs the rhythm hit. Pointer/touch: tap the water to cast or hit; tap gear/perk cards to purchase. During an active rhythm bar, any tap outside pause counts as the rhythm input rather than buying an upgrade.
> * Landscape is the intended desktop and mobile layout. Portrait shows a clear rotate prompt rather than shrinking the timing bar into an unreliable target.
>
> **Session structure and economy targets**
>
> * First 10 seconds: the player casts immediately, catches a common fish with a generous tutorial window, and can afford the first Rod level from that catch.
> * By 30 seconds: 3–4 casts are complete, the streak system is visible, and the first five-level gear milestone triggers a Golden Current.
> * Minutes 1–4: Reel, Lure, and Sonar unlock in sequence; new fish silhouettes and rising values create two smaller surges separated by 30–60 second walls.
> * Minutes 7–9: the first true 60–90 second stall makes the Tide Star preview and prestige button pulse. The first prestige grants enough stars for exactly one perk rank.
> * Later prestige loops target 4–6 minutes. Three prestiges and at least eight total perk ranks unlock a guaranteed Moon Marlin cast around minute 20–25.
> * Landing the Moon Marlin opens a results screen showing total fish, Perfect rate, largest catch, prestiges, and peak cash per cast. It includes Share and "Keep Fishing"; the latter continues in uncapped endless tides.
> * There is no terminal game-over. A missed beat is the short fail state; recasting is available after a 0.7-second line-snap recovery.
>
> Economy generation targets: first purchase in 8–12 seconds; four gear families with 50 useful levels each; per-level costs grow roughly 1.16× within a tier; five-level milestones give 1.8–2.5× effective jumps; major unlocks give 8–12× jumps; each prestige should improve early-run output by at least 4×. Number formatting advances through K/M/B/T and scientific notation only beyond the authored ending. Offline earnings are intentionally zero so active rhythm never becomes irrelevant. Progress auto-saves after catches, purchases, and prestige; New Game requires confirmation and clears cash, gear, perks, records, and prestige count.
>
> **Gameplay systems**
>
> * Fish: Six regular species plus the Moon Marlin. Species differ through value, rarity, sprite silhouette, bite cue, timing-window width, and weight roll; they do not add extra buttons or multi-hit patterns.
> * Rod: multiplies landed-fish base value.
> * Reel: shortens post-catch recovery and modestly widens the Good window, with a hard cap that preserves timing challenge.
> * Lure: raises uncommon/rare fish odds and size multipliers.
> * Sonar: reduces bite waits from the initial 4–13 musical beats toward a four-beat floor.
> * Golden Current: triggered by every five-level milestone, new gear family, and prestige. The next three successful catches use gold-tinted water highlights, a ×3 payout multiplier, the minimum bite wait, and larger number rolls. Misses do not consume a surge catch, preventing a celebration from being lost to one error.
> * Prestige: available once lifetime cash reaches the generated threshold. It resets money, gear, streak, and current surge, while preserving Tide Stars, perks, records, and prestige count. Star gain is previewed before confirmation.
> * Permanent perks: a compact six-tile board with rank caps: stronger global catch value, faster early Sonar, higher Perfect bonus, greater Golden Current multiplier, better rare-fish odds, and starting money. Prices grow about 3× per rank; no branching prerequisite maze.
> * Moon Marlin: unlocked deterministically after the progression target, uses the standard one-press rhythm rule with the narrow rare-fish window, and is guaranteed to bite on the next cast so the climax cannot be delayed by randomness.
>
> **Signature experience**
>
> 1. The wait and bite must be musically connected: ambient water continues during the wait, the bobber dip lands on a beat, and the four-step bar makes the final press readable by both motion and sound.
> 2. Every breakthrough must look and sound like a surge rather than a quiet multiplier change: an odometer-style cash roll, "GOLDEN CURRENT" banner, gold wake behind the bobber, and three visibly boosted catches.
> 3. Prestige must turn a stall into immediate acceleration: after buying a perk, the first three catches arrive at the minimum wait and rapidly repurchase early gear.
>
> **Game feel**
>
> * Cast: 180 ms rod anticipation, 350 ms line arc, bobber squash on contact, two ripple rings, light plop, then recovery to idle.
> * Bite: two small bobber twitches, a 120 ms downward dip, sharp splash, and rhythm-bar snap-in on the beat.
> * Perfect: marker and target overlap flashes white for one frame, 60 ms hit-stop, a small camera bump, bright pixel splash, rising "PERFECT ×1.5," and a pitched bell layered over the beat.
> * Good: smaller blue-white flash and softer bell, without hit-stop.
> * Miss: line becomes taut, snaps into two curling ends, fish silhouette dives, music ducks briefly, and "TOO EARLY" or "TOO LATE" appears before the quick reset.
> * Land: fish follows a short parabolic arc to the dock, weight and value stamp beside it, then cash digits roll upward rather than teleporting. Rare fish use a larger splash and longer 100 ms impact pause.
> * Use the platform FX runtime for semantic splash, impact, number-burst, and camera-response effects rather than unrelated continuous particles.
>
> **Art direction**
>
> * Authentic low-resolution pixel art with readable 1–2 pixel outlines, limited per-sprite palettes, no smooth vector edges, and no high-resolution "pixel-flavored" painting.
> * Medium split: ready-made pixel assets first where they match; generated and deterministically pixelated opaque scene layers and transparent fish/angler sprite sheets for the specific cast; library pixel display font and audio; code only for the timing bar, rolling numbers, line geometry, UI state overlays, and gameplay FX.
> * Palette: deep navy water `#16324F`, teal midtone `#2F7F78`, foam `#B8E0D2`, dusk coral `#FF8A66`, surge gold `#FFD166`, ink `#17202A`, and warm UI cream `#FFF1CC`.
> * Lighting progresses from calm blue dusk to a temporary gold reflection during Golden Current. Fish remain high-contrast against the water; rarity is shown by accent pixels and splash color, never by hue alone.
> * Three parallax layers: gradient sky and blocky clouds, distant shore/reeds, and animated foreground water. Keep the center water uncluttered so the bobber and rhythm bar remain legible.
> * Animation budget: angler idle 4 frames, cast 6, celebrate 4; each fish needs 2-frame swim plus a 4-frame landed flop; bobber and line are separately animated; water uses two alternating tile frames.
> * Avoid dense UI chrome, tiny text, fake scanlines, and screen-wide shake that interferes with timing.
>
> **Audio and UI**
>
> * A looping 100 BPM laid-back chiptune provides an audible pulse without dominating water ambience. Bite count-in clicks, hit bells, splash variations, line snap, cash ticks, upgrade clacks, and a short prestige swell are synchronized to it.
> * Information hierarchy: top-left current money in the largest type; directly below, average cash per cast and streak; top-right Tide Stars and prestige preview; bottom dock four gear cards showing level, next effect, and cost; center reserved for bite cues and timing bar.
> * Affordable upgrades pulse once when they become purchasable, then remain outlined rather than continually flashing. At a stall, the prestige preview replaces generic prompts with the exact stars gained and the strongest affordable perk.
> * Results screen captures the Moon Marlin landing frame and offers Share, Keep Fishing, and confirmed New Game.
>
> **Production priorities**
>
> * P0: one-button cast/wait/bite/timing/catch loop; deterministic money and upgrades; miss recovery; first prestige with persistent perks; crisp scalable pixel presentation.
> * P1: all fish species, generated economy curve, Golden Current celebrations, Moon Marlin ending/results/share, automatic save, desktop and touch input.
> * P2: parallax dusk layers, species-specific splash/audio variations, detailed catch records, subtle water and angler idle animations.
